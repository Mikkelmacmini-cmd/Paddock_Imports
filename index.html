About Paddock Imports

DENVER'S IMPORT EXPERTS

Owner Doug Paddock has been working on cars since his teens and has been servicing BMWs and Audis for nearly 30 years. That passion and expertise became the foundation for Paddock Imports — Denver's most trusted independent import auto repair shop.

Doug built a team of certified automotive service professionals dedicated to maintaining your vehicle at optimal performance levels. Located at 2400 W 7th Ave in Denver, we provide factory-level service with the personalized attention your vehicle deserves.

Step 1: Inspection & Diagnosis

Thorough initial inspection, consultation, and precise computer diagnostics.

Step 2: Parts & Service

OEM-quality parts ordered and installed, or service performed to factory specification.

Step 3: Test Drive Verification

Every vehicle receives a final test drive to confirm the repair meets our standards.

Step 4: Service Summary & Warranty

Full follow-up summary provided with best-in-class warranty information.
